Privacy Policy for DMUC

Last Updated: February 1, 2026

DMUC ("Don't Miss Ur Chance") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data Collection

DMUC identifies job and school-related acceptance emails within your Gmail inbox. To do this, the extension uses the gmail.readonly permission.

2. Data Usage

Email content is analyzed using the Gemini API to identify important updates. This data is processed in real-time. We do not store, collect, or share your email content on any external servers under our control.

3. Data Protection

We do not sell, trade, or rent user data to third parties. All data access is strictly limited to the core functionality of the extension.

4. Contact

If you have questions, please contact us through our GitHub repository support page.
